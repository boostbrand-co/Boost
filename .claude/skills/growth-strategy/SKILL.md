# Growth Strategy

Use this skill for go-to-market plans, acquisition strategy, channel planning, paid media direction, growth roadmaps, launch plans, and performance systems.

## Principle

Growth strategy connects a business model to repeatable customer acquisition and retention.

Do not produce a list of channels without explaining which customer behavior each channel is meant to create, how success is measured, and what must become true before scaling.

## 1. Start from economics and stage

Clarify:

- business model
- revenue event
- average order / contract value
- gross margin when available
- repeat / retention behavior
- sales cycle
- launch stage
- geographic scope
- budget
- team capacity

Do not use mature-company channel assumptions for a pre-launch business.

## 2. Define the growth objective

Examples:

- first 100 qualified leads
- marketplace liquidity
- first repeatable paid acquisition channel
- improve activation
- increase repeat purchase
- reduce CAC
- increase conversion rate
- validate a new market

One plan may have multiple KPIs, but it should have one primary growth problem.

## 3. Funnel / loop

Map the real path:

Awareness → Consideration → Lead / Visit → Conversion → Activation → Retention → Referral / Repeat

For marketplaces, map both sides separately and show the liquidity loop.

## 4. Channel roles

For each channel define:

- audience
- intent level
- job
- offer / message
- expected action
- measurement
- budget or effort
- scale constraint

Examples:

- Search: capture existing intent
- Meta/TikTok: create or re-engage demand
- LinkedIn: targeted B2B prospecting
- SEO/GEO: compound discovery
- Partnerships: batch supply / distribution
- Email: nurture / retention
- PR: credibility + demand + backlinks

Do not assign a channel because “every brand needs it.”

## 5. Message-channel fit

Different channels require different reasons to act.

Map:

- high-intent query → direct solution / proof
- cold social → recognizable problem / curiosity / proof
- outbound → relevance / reason for contact
- retargeting → objection / proof / urgency

Keep offer and landing experience consistent.

## 6. Paid acquisition

Before scaling paid spend define:

- conversion event
- tracking
- creative volume
- landing page
- target CPA/CAC
- attribution limitations
- test budget
- decision rule

Do not calculate ROAS from LTV:CAC or total business revenue without attributable ad revenue.

## 7. Experiment design

Each experiment should state:

- hypothesis
- audience
- variable
- success metric
- minimum evidence needed
- duration / spend
- action if win
- action if fail

Avoid endless A/B tests with no decision threshold.

## 8. Budget logic

When exact forecasts are unavailable, show budget by role and stage rather than pretending precision.

Examples:

- validation spend
- scaling spend
- retargeting
- creative production
- tools
- partnerships

Tie increases to evidence thresholds.

## 9. Measurement

Build a KPI tree:

### Business
Revenue / MRR / orders / active customers

### Acquisition
CAC / CPA / CPL / qualified leads

### Conversion
CVR / booked calls / close rate / activation

### Retention
Repeat rate / churn / renewal / cohort value

### Channel diagnostics
CTR / CPC / CPM / watch time / open rate only when they help explain the main KPI

Do not let channel metrics replace business outcomes.

## 10. Operating cadence

Define:

- weekly review
- creative review
- channel optimization
- monthly strategy
- experiment log
- budget changes
- reporting owner

Growth needs a decision cadence, not only a plan.

## 11. Scale gates

Define what must be true before scaling:

- tracking works
- message converts
- CAC is within threshold
- delivery can handle volume
- retention is acceptable
- creative supply exists

Do not scale spend because early top-line metrics look exciting.

## Acceptance checklist

- Is the primary growth problem clear?
- Does each channel have a job?
- Are conversion events measurable?
- Are economics and stage reflected?
- Are experiments tied to decisions?
- Are paid-media claims attribution-safe?
- Are scale gates explicit?
- Can the team operate the plan weekly?

If not, revise.
