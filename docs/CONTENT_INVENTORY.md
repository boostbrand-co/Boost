### Deliberately not carried over
| Item | Reason |
|---|---|
| "Boost your impact" / "The creative growth agency for brands ready to scale" | Positioning rule: creative growth agency and scale are both on the saturation list |
| "AI-powered execution" | Saturation map rated AI-powered EXTREME (roughly 8 in 10 agency sites use it) |
| "One team for strategy, paid media, content, and design" | Kept as an idea, rewritten as a system with artifacts rather than a claim |
| ROI calculator showing "9.6x return on spend" | Built on an assumed 25 USD cost per lead. Projecting a return the buyer has not earned is exactly the pattern the buyer research shows sophisticated buyers distrust. If a calculator returns, it should compute the client's own numbers, not a promise |
| "12+ industries served" | Vague and unverifiable in its current form. The case library shows 8 industries with named work |
| Strategic partner logos (GROW ME, dmp) | No context or permission status available |
| Contact form service dropdown | Replaced by the situation-led four-step diagnostic |
