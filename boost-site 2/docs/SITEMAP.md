## Why this shape

Situation-first, industry-second. The architecture audit of 79 agency sites found the audience axis
is the biggest separator between strong and thin architectures, while the buyer research found buyers
self-identify by situation rather than by industry. So situations get pages and industry gets filters.

Two page families almost no competitor has: replacing an agency (2 of 79 sites) and in-house team
support (2 of 79). Replacing an agency is also the most common documented purchase trigger.
